# Aissa Com

Aissa Com is a multi-account e-commerce management app that gives shop owners and their team one place to run daily operations. Admins and employees each get their own account, with role-based access so employees have limited permissions. It tracks shop activity end-to-end — orders, products, inventory, purchases, and income — and calculates fair employee rewards based on real contribution.

## Features

- Role-based accounts for admins and employees
- Create, track, and manage customer orders
- Full product catalog with pricing and variants
- Inventory tracking with stock movement logs
- Purchase recording to keep stock accurate
- Income and expense tracking with summaries
- Visual analytics dashboard for shop performance
- Daily task management for the team
- Automatic employee reward calculation, daily and monthly
- Secure, encrypted session storage

## Why It Matters

Aissa Com replaces scattered notebooks, spreadsheets, and shared logins with one organized system. It gives owners full visibility over their business while safely delegating daily work to their team.

## Conclusion

Aissa Com is a reliable, all-in-one tool for shop owners who want organized operations, transparent finances, and a motivated team.

## Tech Stack

Flutter, Dart, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Core, Riverpod, GoRouter, FL Chart, Google Fonts, Flutter Secure Storage, Connectivity Plus

## Problems

1. Orders scattered across notebooks, chats
2. Shared admin logins, no control
3. Manual income tracking, unclear profit
4. No fair employee reward system
5. Untracked inventory caused stock mismatches

## Solutions

1. Centralized order tracking in-app
2. Role-based accounts limit access
3. Automatic income and expense summaries
4. Fair, contribution-based reward calculation
5. Logged movements prevent stock mismatches
